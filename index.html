01Research

Living systems.
Mathematical questions.

My work connects biological mechanisms with the patterns we observe in the ocean. I develop models that explain how organisms respond to their environment and methods that quantify what we can learn from marine data.

Light & phytoplankton

Modelling photosynthetic responses to light, including photoinhibition and environmental variability, to understand their consequences for marine primary production.

PhotophysiologyPrimary production
Read the photoinhibition paper

Ocean carbon & uncertainty

Developing statistical and machine-learning approaches for surface-ocean pCO₂ reconstruction, with a focus on predictive uncertainty and probabilistic methods.

Machine learningCarbon cycling
Explore related publications

Biological dynamics & memory

Using fractional calculus and differential equations to study how past conditions influence microbial growth, ecological interactions, and complex biological dynamics.

Mechanistic modelsFractional calculus
Read the microbial memory paper
